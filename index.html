typing tips

touch typing: type without looking at the keyboard. each finger owns specific keys. f and j have tactile bumps to orient your hands.

home row: return fingers to asdf / jkl; after every keystroke. this is the foundation of muscle memory.

accuracy first: slow down until errors are near zero, then speed up. fixing mistakes mid-word costs more time than the error itself.

chunking: read 2–3 words ahead. legal phrases like "proximate cause" become single chunks with practice.
